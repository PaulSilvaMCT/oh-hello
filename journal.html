September 2026 Beginning

Why Oh, Hello exists.

Andrea has spent her life surrounded by family and people who understand that she needs support navigating many ordinary parts of everyday life.

Over the past several years, obtaining appropriate services for her has become increasingly difficult. Requests, assessments, authorizations, provider changes and appeals have accumulated while Andrea's daily needs have continued.

Oh, Hello was created so Andrea's story would no longer exist only inside forms, telephone calls, insurance files and government records.

This is a place where Andrea can be seen first as a person.

A photograph of Andrea will go here. This space can later hold a portrait or an ordinary moment from Andrea's life.
September 2026 Care

Presence is not the same as care.

During a recent weekend shift, Andrea's brother came downstairs without being noticed by the aide working with her.

He reports observing the aide scrolling continuously on her phone for approximately fifteen minutes while Andrea sat nearby eating breakfast.

During that period, he observed no meaningful interaction between the aide and Andrea.

After raising his concerns, he ended the shift, contacted the provider agency and requested a replacement.

This entry describes a firsthand family observation. The purpose of documenting it is not to publicly target an individual worker. It illustrates why meaningful care involves more than the physical presence of another person.
September 2026 Advocacy

Asking the world to notice.

After a request for additional home-care assistance was denied, Andrea's family began reaching beyond the ordinary administrative channels.

Disability-rights organizations, aging advocates, elected officials, television newsrooms, print media and community organizations were contacted.

The message was simple:

Andrea cannot effectively advocate for herself. Someone has to make sure she is heard.

Oh, Hello grew from that effort.

A record with a purpose

Documentation matters.

Some journal entries will also help create a contemporaneous record of Andrea's functional and supervision needs.

How we will document care incidents

When an incident helps explain Andrea's support needs, the private record should capture the date and time, what happened, whether an aide was present, what risk existed, what intervention was needed and what happened next.

The public journal may then tell the same story in a way that protects Andrea's dignity and unnecessary private details.

Not every private care note belongs on the internet. The purpose is to document what matters without turning Andrea's most personal moments into public property.

Andrea's life is evidence.
But Andrea is more than evidence.

Photographs, short videos, family stories, care observations and advocacy updates will be added as Oh, Hello develops.
