How
You Can Help.

Andrea does not need sympathy from a distance. She needs people willing to help move something forward.

Sometimes that means making a phone call. Sometimes it means sharing a connection. Sometimes it means helping one more person understand who Andrea is and what she needs.

“One useful connection can matter more than a thousand expressions of concern.”

The goal is not attention for its own sake. The goal is adequate care.

Ways to help

You may know someone we do not.

Oh, Hello was created partly because years of ordinary channels have not produced a sustainable solution. Reaching the right person may change that.

Share

Help Andrea's story travel farther.

Share Oh-Hello.blog with people who may understand disability services, home care, Medicaid, advocacy, elder care, journalism, law or government.

A thoughtful introduction to one relevant person is more useful than indiscriminate sharing.

Advocacy

Connect us with disability advocates.

We are particularly interested in people who understand developmental disability services, Medicaid home care, appeals, fair hearings, person-centered planning and long-term support systems.

Government

Help an elected official actually look at the case.

Legislative offices often have constituent-service staff who can communicate with agencies and health plans. We welcome help reaching people willing to examine Andrea's situation as an individual.

Media

Help a journalist understand the larger story.

Andrea's experience raises questions about home-care authorization, caregiver continuity, administrative process, family burden and what happens when a vulnerable adult cannot effectively advocate for herself.

Professional Help

Share expertise.

Attorneys, social workers, care managers, physicians, disability-service professionals and others with relevant experience may be able to identify options we have missed.

Care Resources

Help us find sustainable support.

We welcome information about qualified caregivers, respite programs, home-care resources and services designed for adults with developmental disabilities.

A special need

Respite matters.

A safe weekend should not be impossible.

Andrea's family has been filling the gaps in her care for years.

Her brother and his wife, Marina, have not had a weekend away together in approximately four to five years. That is not because they do not want one. It is because Andrea cannot simply be left without appropriate support.

If you know of reliable respite programs, short-term caregiving options, disability respite services or organizations that help families arrange safe temporary coverage, that information could make an immediate difference.

What we are not asking

We are not asking the public to replace the system.

Andrea is seeking appropriate professional services through the systems that exist to provide them.

Public attention and community support should not become an excuse for government programs, insurers or care organizations to avoid their responsibilities.

Our first goal remains simple: a safe, sustainable, person-centered care plan for Andrea.

If the system still fails

A financial safety net may become necessary.

Fundraising is a contingency, not the first solution.

If appropriate services cannot be secured quickly enough, Oh, Hello may eventually include a public fundraising effort to help pay for additional professional caregiving, respite and other disability-related support.

Any fundraising structure will be reviewed carefully before launch so that assistance is handled appropriately and does not unintentionally interfere with benefits or services Andrea depends upon.

If that becomes necessary, the purpose and use of funds will be explained clearly here.

For professionals and organizations

If you can help solve something, please reach out.

We are particularly interested in substantive assistance, referrals, advocacy, media inquiries and connections to organizations capable of affecting Andrea's care.
